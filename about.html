Our story

Why MINT exists

Most physical therapy clinics run on volume — several patients rotating through the same hour, most of the actual session handed off to an aide or a machine. MINT was founded on the opposite premise: a single therapist, a single patient, for the entire visit.

That means a real evaluation before treatment begins, a plan that's explained rather than assumed, and a therapist who actually remembers your case from one visit to the next — because they're the only one who's touched it.

It's slower to scale. It's also the only version of physical therapy we're willing to put our name on.

Dr. Brad, DPT — Founder & Lead Physical Therapist

What guides the work

Four things we won't compromise on.

Real evaluation

Every plan starts with a full assessment — never a default protocol assigned before we've seen you move.

One therapist

The person who evaluates you is the person who treats you, visit after visit.

Plain explanation

You'll always know why a specific exercise or technique is part of your plan.

Honest timelines

We tell you what recovery actually looks like — not what's easiest to hear.
